# Devine-moi 1.1.0 (3) – Notes for App Review

## Purpose and audience
Devine-moi is a free, offline French word game with two daily puzzles:
- **Le mot du jour**: guess a 5-letter French word in 6 tries (coloured tiles show correct / misplaced letters), plus an unlimited **Entraînement** (practice) mode with 5-, 6- or 7-letter words.
- **Le mot brouillé**: unscramble letters into a word; each word found adds one letter (4 up to 8 letters), then a 9-letter bonus word.

Audience: French-speaking adults and teenagers who enjoy word puzzles. No ads, no account, no purchases, no data collection.

## How to use the main features
No login required — the app has no account; the « Sign-in required » option has been unticked.
1. Launch the app: the home screen shows today's two games.
2. Tap **Mot du jour**. Type a 5-letter word on the on-screen keyboard and press Enter. Tiles turn colour; repeat until solved or 6 tries used. A result sheet appears (stats, **Partager** = iOS share sheet, link to the definition on Wiktionary, opened in Safari).
3. Use the **Mot du jour / Entraînement** tabs to switch to unlimited practice.
4. Tap the title plaque to return home, then tap **Mot brouillé**. Tap letters to build the word; **Mélanger**, **Effacer** and **Indice** help.
5. Top-bar icons: **?** (how to play), **Statistiques**, **Réglages** (sounds, music, hard mode, high contrast, keyboard layout, practice word length, credits), music toggle.
All progress and settings are stored locally on the device only.

## External services
None. The app makes no network requests: no backend, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, authentication, payment or AI service. All words, sounds, music and fonts are bundled in the app. The only outbound actions are user-initiated: tapping a Wiktionary or source-code link opens Safari, and **Partager** opens the system share sheet. No permissions are requested.

## Regional differences
The app works identically in every country and region; there is no region-specific content or behaviour. The user interface is in French only. The keyboard layout (AZERTY/QWERTZ/QWERTY) is chosen automatically from the device locale and can be changed in Réglages.
